## Concept acknowledgements

The Cowork 2.0 canvas takes product ideas that these projects show publicly. No source code was read or copied from either one.

- [nodeterm](https://github.com/eneskirca/nodeterm) (BUSL-1.1): the idea of terminals as nodes on a canvas. We did not read its source; the canvas is our own implementation on `@xyflow/react`.
- [OpenMausBot](https://github.com/milind-soni/OpenMausBot) (Apache-2.0): the idea of an agent roster and an approval flow. Its name is not used for any brand or feature.
